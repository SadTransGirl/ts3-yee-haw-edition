Rope MF (RopeMF.ttf)

"1.01 Rope MF (c)1993 By Richard William Mueller" (the notice inside the font file).

Released as freeware in 1993. Font catalogues such as FontSpace list it as
"100% Free" for personal and commercial use. It's included here unmodified
(only the file name was shortened from rope.ttf) and is not sold; thanks to
Richard William Mueller for it.

The launcher embeds it for its rope-lettered section headings.
